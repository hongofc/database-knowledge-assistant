# SOP CAL-01 — Measurement Equipment Calibration

_Sample data for the Factory Knowledge startup kit. Fictional — replace with your real calibration procedure._

**SOP ID:** CAL-01 · **Owner:** Quality Engineering · **Rev:** 2.2

## 1. Why calibration matters

A measurement is only trustworthy if the gauge is calibrated and traceable. Using an
out-of-cal gauge can pass bad parts or scrap good ones. Every controlled gauge has a
unique **CAL ID** and a calibration sticker showing the due date.

## 2. Calibration intervals

| Instrument | Interval | Standard used |
|------------|----------|---------------|
| Digital caliper | 12 months | Grade 0 gauge blocks |
| Micrometer | 12 months | Gauge blocks + optical flat |
| Bore gauge | 6 months | Setting ring |
| Torque wrench | 6 months | Torque analyzer |
| Profilometer | 12 months | Reference specimen |
| CMM | 12 months (annual) | Calibrated artifact, external lab |

Intervals shorten if a gauge is dropped, suspect, or used heavily.

## 3. Daily user check (before use)

1. Confirm the **calibration sticker is in date** (CAL ID and due date).
2. Inspect for damage — chipped jaws, sticky movement, cracked display.
3. Zero the instrument on a known reference (e.g. close caliper jaws → reads 0.00).
4. If it won't zero or is past due, **quarantine it** with a "DO NOT USE" tag and
   get a replacement.

## 4. Caliper calibration (example procedure)

1. Clean the jaws and gauge blocks; allow to stabilize at room temperature.
2. Verify zero with jaws closed.
3. Measure gauge blocks at **5 points** across the range (e.g. 10, 25, 50, 75,
   100 mm).
4. Record actual vs nominal; deviation must be within **±0.02 mm**.
5. If within tolerance, apply a new sticker with the next due date. If not, tag out
   for repair or replacement.

## 5. Records & traceability

All calibration results are logged in the calibration register with the standard
used and its certificate number, maintaining traceability to national standards.
Records are retained per the quality manual.

## 6. Out-of-tolerance gauge found

If a gauge is found out of tolerance, the quality engineer reviews **what was
measured with it since the last good calibration** to assess impact on shipped
product. This is why prompt reporting matters.

## 7. Escalation

Past-due, damaged, or suspect gauge, or no replacement available: tag it "DO NOT
USE" and contact the **Quality Engineer** (ext. 4700). Never assume a value or use a
gauge "just this once."
