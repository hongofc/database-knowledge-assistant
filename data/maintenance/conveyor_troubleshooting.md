# Belt Conveyor BX-200 — Troubleshooting Guide

_Sample data for the Factory Knowledge startup kit. Fictional — replace with your real guide._

**Asset ID:** BX-200 · **Drive:** 3 kW gearmotor · **Belt:** 600 mm PVC

## Safety first

Before any inspection that requires reaching into the conveyor, apply **lockout/
tagout** (Safety SOP LOTO-01) and confirm the drive cannot start. Many conveyor
injuries happen during "quick checks" with the power still live.

## 1. Belt mistracking / drift (alarm C-101)

**Symptoms:** Belt runs to one side, edges fray, material spills.
**Steps:**
1. Lockout/tagout the drive.
2. Inspect rollers for build-up or damage; clean or replace.
3. Adjust the take-up bolts **a quarter turn at a time, evenly on both sides**.
4. Run briefly and observe; repeat small adjustments until centered.
5. Over-tightening causes bearing wear — only tension enough to stop slip.

## 2. Belt slipping

**Symptoms:** Belt stalls under load, drive runs but belt barely moves.
**Likely causes:** Low tension, worn drive lagging, overload.
**Steps:** Check load is within rating, increase take-up tension slightly, inspect
drive pulley lagging for wear, confirm material isn't jammed at the infeed.

## 3. Unusual noise or vibration

**Symptoms:** Grinding, squealing, or knocking from the drive or idlers.
**Likely causes:** Failing roller bearing, misalignment, debris.
**Steps:** Lockout/tagout, spin each idler by hand to find a rough or seized
bearing, replace the affected roller, and **log the vibration observation** in the
maintenance log so trends can be tracked.

## 4. Motor overcurrent trip (alarm C-205)

**Symptoms:** Conveyor stops, panel shows C-205.
**Steps:**
1. Lockout/tagout.
2. Clear any jam at the infeed or discharge.
3. Spin rollers by hand — a seized bearing causes overcurrent.
4. Check the gearmotor for overheating; let it cool.
5. Reset; if it trips again, **escalate to the maintenance supervisor** — do not
   keep resetting a tripping motor.

## 5. Emergency stop pressed (alarm C-301)

Identify why the E-stop was pressed and clear the hazard **before** resetting.
Twist-release the pressed button, then reset at the panel. Never reset blind.

## 6. Preventive checks

| Interval | Check |
|----------|-------|
| Daily | Belt tracking, unusual noise, guards in place |
| Weekly | Roller condition, take-up tension, clean debris |
| Monthly | Gearmotor oil level, belt wear, bolt torque |
| 1,000 h | Replace worn idlers, inspect drive lagging |

## 7. Escalation

If a fault recurs after the steps above, tag the conveyor out of service and raise a
work order to the **maintenance supervisor** (ext. 4500).
