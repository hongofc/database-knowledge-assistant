# Maintenance Log — Line 2

This log records corrective and preventive maintenance for Line 2 assets (VMX-500
CNC mill, BX-200 conveyor). The assistant can cite these entries when a recurring
issue has been seen before. Newest entries first.

## 2026-06-10 · VMX-500 · Corrective
- **Symptom:** Recurring alarm E-204 (spindle overload) twice in one shift.
- **Found:** Spindle drive belt (VMX-BELT-11) glazed and slipping under load.
- **Action:** Replaced drive belt, set tension to spec, ran warm-up. No recurrence.
- **Technician:** R. Okafor · **Downtime:** 95 min

## 2026-06-03 · BX-200 · Corrective
- **Symptom:** Alarm C-205 motor overcurrent; belt stalling at infeed.
- **Found:** Seized idler roller bearing causing drag.
- **Action:** Replaced idler roller, checked adjacent bearings, cleared C-205.
- **Technician:** L. Marín · **Downtime:** 60 min

## 2026-05-28 · VMX-500 · Preventive
- **Task:** 500-hour service — greased X/Y/Z ballscrew bearings (NLGI 2), topped up
  way oil, replaced coolant filter, verified coolant at 7%.
- **Technician:** R. Okafor · **Downtime:** 40 min (planned)

## 2026-05-15 · BX-200 · Corrective
- **Symptom:** Belt drift to operator side (C-101), material spillage.
- **Found:** Uneven take-up tension after a prior roller change.
- **Action:** Re-tensioned take-up evenly, cleaned rollers, verified tracking.
- **Technician:** L. Marín · **Downtime:** 35 min

## 2026-05-02 · VMX-500 · Corrective
- **Symptom:** Alarm E-110 low lubricant during startup.
- **Found:** Way-lube reservoir low; minor weep at a lube line fitting.
- **Action:** Topped up ISO VG 68, tightened fitting, monitored — no further loss.
- **Technician:** R. Okafor · **Downtime:** 20 min

## 2026-04-20 · VMX-500 · Corrective
- **Symptom:** Poor surface finish on aluminium parts.
- **Found:** Worn end mill and coolant concentration low (4%).
- **Action:** Replaced tool, corrected coolant to 7%, re-ran first article — passed.
- **Technician:** S. Patel · **Downtime:** 30 min

## Recurring-issue notes
- **E-204 on VMX-500** has correlated with **drive-belt wear**; check belt tension
  first when it appears (see 2026-06-10).
- **C-101 belt drift** often follows roller changes — always re-tension evenly.
