# SOP LOTO-01 — Lockout/Tagout (Control of Hazardous Energy)

_Sample data for the Factory Knowledge startup kit. Fictional — replace with your real, compliant SOP._

**SOP ID:** LOTO-01 · **Owner:** Safety & Compliance · **Rev:** 4.0

## 1. Purpose & scope

This procedure prevents the unexpected start-up or release of stored energy
(electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal) during servicing or
maintenance. It applies to **all** machines on the floor, including the VMX-500 mill
and BX-200 conveyor. **No maintenance inside a guard or enclosure may begin until
LOTO is complete.**

## 2. Who may perform LOTO

Only **authorized employees** trained in this SOP may apply locks. Each authorized
employee uses their **own uniquely keyed padlock** and a signed tag. Never use
someone else's lock or remove another person's lock.

## 3. The 6 steps

1. **Prepare** — identify all energy sources for the equipment (check the machine's
   energy-source list on the panel placard).
2. **Notify** — tell affected operators the machine is being shut down and why.
3. **Shut down** — stop the machine using its normal controls.
4. **Isolate** — turn off and disconnect each energy source (main disconnect, air
   supply valve, hydraulic shutoff).
5. **Lock & tag** — apply your padlock and tag to each isolation point. The tag
   states your name, date, and reason.
6. **Verify zero energy** — attempt a normal start (it must not run), bleed
   residual air/hydraulic pressure, and confirm with a meter where electrical.

> **Golden rule:** Try to start the machine after locking out. If it could still
> start, you are NOT locked out.

## 4. Removing locks (restoring energy)

1. Confirm tools are removed and guards are reinstalled.
2. Ensure all personnel are clear of the machine.
3. Each person removes **only their own** lock and tag.
4. Notify operators, then restore energy and test.

## 5. Stored-energy specifics

- **Pneumatic (6 bar air):** close the supply valve and bleed the line before work.
- **Hydraulic:** relieve pressure at the designated bleed point.
- **Spindle/rotating mass:** wait for complete stop; do not hand-brake.
- **Thermal:** allow hot surfaces to cool or wear rated gloves.

## 6. If you find a machine locked out

Do not attempt to start it or remove the lock. Find the person named on the tag. If
a lock must be removed and the owner is unreachable, **only the safety officer** may
authorize removal following the lock-removal procedure.

## 7. Escalation

Questions, missing energy-source placards, or unsafe conditions: **stop work** and
contact the **Safety Officer** (ext. 4911) immediately.
