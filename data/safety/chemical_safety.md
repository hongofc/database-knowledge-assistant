# Chemical & Hazardous Material Safety

_Sample data for the Factory Knowledge startup kit. Fictional — replace with your real chemical safety program._

**Doc:** SAFE-CHEM-03 · **Owner:** Safety & Compliance · **Rev:** 1.4

## 1. Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

Every chemical on site has an **SDS** in the binder at the chemical store and in the
digital SDS portal. **Read the SDS before first use** — it lists hazards, required
PPE, storage, and spill response. If a chemical has no SDS, do not use it; report it
to the Safety Officer.

## 2. Common chemicals on the floor

| Chemical | Use | Key hazard | PPE |
|----------|-----|-----------|-----|
| Cutting coolant concentrate | Mixed for CNC coolant | Skin/eye irritant | Goggles, nitrile gloves |
| Way oil ISO VG 68 | Machine lubrication | Slip hazard, mild irritant | Gloves |
| Parts-cleaning solvent | Degreasing | Flammable, vapor | Goggles, neoprene gloves, respirator, ventilation |
| Aerosol penetrating oil | Freeing parts | Flammable propellant | Goggles, gloves |

## 3. Storage

- Store flammables in the **flammable cabinet**, away from ignition sources.
- Keep containers **closed and labeled**; never decant into unlabeled bottles.
- Separate incompatible chemicals per the SDS (e.g. acids away from solvents).
- Keep aisles and eyewash stations clear at all times.

## 4. Spill response (small spill, < 5 L)

1. **Alert** people nearby and remove ignition sources if flammable.
2. Put on the PPE listed in the SDS.
3. Contain with the spill kit (socks/absorbent pads) — stop it reaching drains.
4. Absorb, bag the waste as hazardous, and label it.
5. Ventilate the area and clean the surface.
6. Log the spill and restock the spill kit.

## 5. Large spill or any spill reaching a drain

**Do not attempt to clean it yourself.** Evacuate the immediate area, prevent
others entering, and call the **Safety Officer (ext. 4911)** and follow the
emergency procedures. Drains connect to the environment — a reportable event.

## 6. Skin / eye contact

- **Eyes:** flush at the eyewash station for **at least 15 minutes**, hold eyelids
  open, and seek first aid. Do not rub.
- **Skin:** remove contaminated clothing, wash with soap and water per the SDS.
- **Inhalation:** move to fresh air; if symptoms persist, seek first aid.
Always report exposures and complete an incident report.

## 7. Waste disposal

Chemical waste goes in the **labeled hazardous-waste drums** in the chemical store —
never in general trash or down a drain. Different waste streams must not be mixed.

## 8. Escalation

Unsure about a chemical, missing an SDS, or facing a spill beyond a small one:
**stop and contact the Safety Officer** (ext. 4911).
