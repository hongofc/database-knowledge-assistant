# PPE Requirements by Area

_Sample data for the Factory Knowledge startup kit. Fictional — replace with your real PPE policy._

**Doc:** SAFE-PPE-02 · **Owner:** Safety & Compliance · **Rev:** 2.1

## 1. General rules

PPE is the **last line of defense** — guards, procedures, and ventilation come
first. Inspect PPE before each use; replace anything cracked, torn, or expired.
Required PPE is posted at the entrance to each area; the table below is the master.

## 2. PPE matrix

| Area / task | Eye | Hearing | Hand | Foot | Other |
|-------------|-----|---------|------|------|-------|
| General shop floor | Safety glasses | — | — | Safety shoes | Hi-vis vest |
| CNC machining (VMX-500) | Safety glasses | If >85 dB | Cut-resistant when handling tools | Safety shoes | No gloves near rotating spindle |
| Grinding / deburring | Face shield + glasses | Earplugs | Cut-resistant | Safety shoes | Dust mask |
| Chemical handling | Chemical goggles | — | Nitrile/neoprene per SDS | Safety shoes | Apron; respirator per SDS |
| Welding | Welding helmet (shade per process) | Earplugs | Welding gauntlets | Safety shoes | FR clothing |
| Conveyor maintenance | Safety glasses | — | Gloves (not near nip points) | Safety shoes | — |

> **Critical:** Never wear gloves, loose sleeves, jewelry, or a lanyard near a
> rotating spindle or conveyor nip point — they can pull your hand in. Tie back
> long hair.

## 3. Hearing protection

Required where noise exceeds **85 dB(A)** (8-hour average). The machining and
grinding areas are posted hearing-protection zones. Use earplugs or earmuffs rated
for the posted level.

## 4. Respiratory protection

Required for tasks generating dust, mist, or vapor as specified in the relevant SDS
(see `chemical_safety.md`). Respirators must be fit-tested; facial hair that breaks
the seal is not permitted with tight-fitting respirators.

## 5. Foot & body protection

Steel/composite-toe safety shoes are mandatory **everywhere** on the production
floor. Hi-vis vests are required in areas with moving vehicles (forklifts, AGVs).

## 6. Getting and replacing PPE

Collect standard PPE from the PPE station near the floor entrance. For specialized
PPE (respirators, welding gear) or replacements, see your supervisor. Damaged PPE
must be replaced before starting work — never improvise.

## 7. Escalation

If you are unsure what PPE a task requires, or the right PPE is unavailable,
**stop and ask the Safety Officer** (ext. 4911) before proceeding.
